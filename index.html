What we don't do

  • We don't require you to create an account or sign in.
  • We don't operate our own backend servers, and none of the Apps upload your photos, recordings, drawings, or documents anywhere - processing happens on your device, and files stay on your device unless you explicitly use the Apps' own "share" or "save" action to send them somewhere yourself.
  • We don't sell your data to anyone.

Permissions, explained

Each App only requests the specific device permissions it needs for its one core feature, listed in the table above. A permission is only used for the purpose stated in that App's own in-app explanation, shown before the system asks you to grant it. You can review or revoke any permission at any time in your device's Settings > Apps.

Advertising

These Apps are free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob. To show ads, Google's AdMob SDK may collect and process the following on our behalf:

  • Advertising identifiers (such as your Android Advertising ID)
  • Coarse device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, general region) used for ad delivery and fraud prevention
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks) used for ad performance measurement

We do not control how Google collects or uses this data beyond what's needed to serve ads in the App. You can review Google's own practices at policies.google.com/privacy, and see AdMob's specific data disclosure at support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543. You can opt out of personalized/interest-based advertising in your device's Settings > Google > Ads, or by resetting/deleting your advertising ID.

Times Tables Practice is designed for children practicing multiplication. In line with Google Play's Families Policy, ads shown in that specific App are served in a non-personalized, child-appropriate configuration - no ad personalization based on user profiles or cross-app behavior, and no ad formats designed for adult audiences. If you believe personalized ads are appearing in this App, please contact us using the details below.

Data we do NOT collect

Beyond the ad-serving data described above, we do not collect: your name, email address, precise location, contacts, health data, financial information, or the content of anything you create, record, draw, or capture in these Apps. Local files created by the Apps (recordings, saved signatures, compressed media, dream journal entries, scan history) are stored only on your own device, in the App's own private storage or wherever you explicitly chose to save them.

Children's privacy

Times Tables Practice may be used by children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children through any of our Apps. Ad serving in that App follows Google Play's Families Policy as described above. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

Data security

Since none of the Apps transmit your files or content to any server we operate, the primary security boundary is your own device. We recommend keeping your device's OS updated and using a screen lock.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Apps evolve. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of an App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
